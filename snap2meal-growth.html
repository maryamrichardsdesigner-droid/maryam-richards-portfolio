POST THE PROBLEM · BUILDING IN PUBLIC AS @REGULAMOMMA

THE CONTENT ENGINE.

Every post opens on a real problem at home, then shows the fix. The app only appears as the answer.
THE HOOK · TIKTOK + INSTAGRAM REELS

NOBODY WANTS A RECIPE APP.
THEY WANT 6:47PM SOLVED.

Every piece of creative opens on the problem, in a real kitchen. The app is never the hook — it’s the answer at second five. These six are made and ready to post.
“What if you knew what to cook daily with what you already have?”
The whole account in one line. Every other hook is a smaller version of this one.
“Dinner? Hungry?”
Two words, one second. Nothing to read, nothing to decode — the fastest scroll-stopper of the set.
“Have random ingredients?”
Names the fridge, not the app. You answer it in your head before you decide to keep watching.
“5-min breakfast idea?”
Time is the promise. A number in the first frame sets the price of watching.
“15-min quick dinner”
Same trick, worst hour of the day. This is the one to spend money behind if it works organically.
“Dessert?”
Cupboard leftovers into pudding. Widens the app past dinner without a single new feature.

STAGE 01 · SOCIAL · @REGULAMOMMA
THE PROBLEM IS THE HOOK.
Nobody searches for a recipe app. They do know 5:47pm with a full fridge and no idea what to make. So every post leads with that feeling, and Snap2Meal only shows up as the fix.
→ DOWNLOADS
STAGE 02 · APP STORE
NEVER KNOW WHAT TO COOK.
The listing makes one promise before any feature: point your camera at what you already have and get three meal ideas in seconds. Everything else comes second.
→ DOWNLOADS
STAGE 03 · FIRST OPEN
STRANGERS DON’T FORGIVE.
Launching changed who was looking. Strangers don’t forgive what you’ve stopped noticing, so the first run was rewritten before the public ever saw it.
→ FIRST SNAP
STAGE 04 · FIRST SNAP
ONE PHOTO, NO TYPING.
Snap or upload what’s in the fridge and the app works out the ingredients. No list to type at the end of a long day.
→ FIRST SNAP
STAGE 05 · THREE MEALS
THREE, NEVER THIRTY.
Every snap costs a vision call — and thirty options was the original problem. Three tailored ideas you can actually make right now.
→ RECIPE COOKED
STAGE 06 · COOK
BUILT FOR A REAL DINNER.
Clear step-by-step recipes with toddler-friendly adaptations and macros — made for the person the post was about.
→ RECIPE COOKED→ BACK IN WEEK TWO
STAGE 07 · SHARE
EVERY SHARE IS A FREE AD.
I turned every share into a free ad. A meal worth making is worth sending — and it carries Snap2Meal with it. The funnel’s exit feeds its entrance.
