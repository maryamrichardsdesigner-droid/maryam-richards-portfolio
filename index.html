ALMOSAFER · SENIOR PRODUCT DESIGNER

Selected work at my most recent company.

Two decisions on the same surface — the fare a traveller picks, then what they add to it. Someone else’s roadmap, real revenue pressure, and eight carriers that each bundle differently. Each started with a call I had to defend, so that’s what leads.

The shipped flow. The cheapest fare arrives selected.
Almosafer
SENIOR PRODUCT DESIGNER · FLIGHT BOOKING · 8 CARRIERS

“The cheapest fare stays selected.”

Revenue wanted the upgrade pre-ticked. I refused the dark pattern and argued the harder position: travellers were losing to airline sites because they couldn’t compare, not because they were cheap. So I made eight carriers’ bundles comparable on six attributes — and let people choose up on purpose.

Who I worked with: product, engineering and the design team.
What it cost: normalising eight carriers meant dropping some carrier-specific perks, and full fare rules moved one tap deeper. I took clarity over completeness.

The questions I keep coming back to
01

What are we actually trying to do?

Before jumping to solutions, I want the real goal — what success looks like for the user, the business and the people behind the request.

02

What’s making this harder than it needs to be?

I look for the real constraint. Sometimes it’s technical, sometimes behavioural — and sometimes we’re solving the wrong problem entirely.

03

How do we know it worked?

Outcomes, not prettier screens. Real change in behaviour, clarity, confidence or revenue — measured, not assumed.

FitConnectA MARKETPLACE FOR PERSONAL TRAINING
DESIGNED & BUILT BY ME · LIVE ON THE WEB

Book a personal trainer the way you book anything else.

What it is: an app where you find verified personal trainers near you, see their price up front, and book and pay inside the app — plus a separate business tool where trainers manage their profile, availability and bookings. Two apps, one codebase, built in React Native on Firebase.

Why it exists: booking a trainer ran through Instagram DMs and bank transfers. No price to compare, no proof anyone was qualified, no protection if it went wrong. The insight: choice was never the bottleneck — people want to trust the one they pick.

CUT
Group classes, nutrition plans, a social feedI wrote the non-goals down first, and that list did more scoping than the roadmap.
CUT
Ten screens of onboardingShow the product, then ask. Ten screens became four.
KEPT
Human-reviewed verificationA slower path to a live profile, in exchange for a claim competitors could not make.
