05 — The tradeoffs

Every choice had a cost.

Designing fare upsell inside an OTA means holding tensions honestly — not pretending they don't exist.

Aggregation vs accuracy

Every airline structures bundles differently. Normalizing 8 carriers into one comparable model meant simplifying some carrier-specific perks — a loss we accepted for clarity.

Revenue vs trust

Upsell pressure invited pre-selected upgrades. I refused the dark pattern: Saver stays the default, and higher fares must be an informed, deliberate choice.

Detail vs decision speed

Full fare rules overwhelm on mobile. I committed us to six comparable attributes up front; edge-case rules live one tap deeper — faster decisions, slightly less exhaustive.
