Almosafer — Fare Families

“The cheapest fare stays selected.”

Customers were leaving for airline sites for refundable fares and mileage upgrades. Revenue wanted the upgrade pre-ticked; I refused the dark pattern. Across three shipped phases I made eight carriers’ fares comparable on six attributes, so travellers choose up on purpose. Worked with product, engineering and the design team.

Snap2Meal

Snap your fridge, get dinner sorted.

The 6pm question, every night: what's actually in the fridge, and what can I cook with it? Snap a photo, get AI-matched meal ideas in seconds, then a real plan built from real groceries — not a recipe blog fantasy.

VALIDATED WITHOther women doing the same 6pm fridge stare. They confirmed it wasn't a recipe problem — it was one decision too many at the end of a long day. That cut the MVP down to snap → three options → cook.
